This graph reveals the direct trade-off between your screen time and sleep, when one rises, the other often falls. Seeing these patterns helps you spot sleep-stealing habits and identify weeks when better balance boosted your energy. Use these insights to build a nighttime routine that works for you, not against you. Hover over the lines to revist your stress level and screentime hours for specfic days!
